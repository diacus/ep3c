#+TITLE: LaTeX settings
#+AUTHOR: Diacus Mágnuz
#+STARTUP: latexpreview overview

For $\TeX$ and $\LaTeX$ use *AUCTeX*, a program offering many featues
and configuration options. Take a look to its [[https://www.gnu.org/software/auctex/manual/auctex.html#Editing-Facilities][quick start]] guide for
details.
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (use-package tex
    :ensure auctex
    :config
    (setq-default TeX-master nil)
    (setq TeX-auto-save t)
    (setq TeX-parse-self t))
#+end_src

* Filling
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (add-hook 'LaTeX-mode-hook #'turn-on-auto-fill)
#+end_src
