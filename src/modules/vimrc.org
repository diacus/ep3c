#+AUTHOR: Diacus Magnuz

[[https://github.com/mcandre/vimrc-mode][vimrc-mode]] is a major mode for Vim's vimscript: it provides font
locking for =.vimrc= and =.vim= files.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (use-package vimrc-mode
    :ensure t
    :mode (("\\.vim\\(rc\\)?\\'" . vimrc-mode)
           ("_vimrc\\'" . vimrc-mode)))
#+end_src
