#+AUTHOR: Diacus Mágnuz
#+STARTUP: overview

Instead of the default init screen, check out this [[https://github.com/emacs-dashboard/emacs-dashboard?tab=readme-ov-file#emacs-dashboard][Emacs Dashboard]].
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (use-package dashboard
    :ensure t
    :after projectile
    :config
    (setq dashboard-startup-banner 'logo
          dashboard-items '((bookmarks . 5)
  			  (recents   . 5)
  			  (projects  . 5)
  			  (registers . 5))
  	dashboard-center-content t
  	dashboard-vertically-center-content t
  	)
    (dashboard-setup-startup-hook))
#+end_src

When running on daemon mode, switch to =*dashboard*= buffer on every new
frame created.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (setq initial-buffer-choice
        (lambda ()
  	(get-buffer-create dashboard-buffer-name)))
#+end_src
